Can I get an initial necklace before Christmas?

Order by December 10. Every piece is made to order in about 5–8 business days and ships with free tracked delivery.

A 16 inch close fit or an 18 inch classic fit chain.

Which metals can I choose?

925 sterling silver, 9K gold, 14K gold and 18K gold.

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp for a letter that is not listed, a different chain length or a custom design.

Every piece is made to order and usually ready to ship in about 5–8 business days. Delivery is free worldwide with tracking.
