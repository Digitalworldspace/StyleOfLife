Our Story

Style Of Life started with one idea: fine jewelry should feel personal, not mass-produced. Every piece is made to order, designed to be worn daily and kept for a lifetime.

Orders are placed through Etsy, backed by Etsy's buyer protection and secure checkout, with sizing and stone details confirmed personally over WhatsApp before your piece is made — and we personally pack, check, and ship every order ourselves.

— Prince Akbari, Founder
