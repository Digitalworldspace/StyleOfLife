Moissanite vs Lab Grown Diamond

Moissanite and lab grown diamond are both grown in a laboratory, but they are different materials. A lab grown diamond is a diamond (pure carbon), while moissanite is silicon carbide, which is a little softer than diamond and shows more rainbow-colored flashes of light.

What is the real difference between the two stones?

A lab grown diamond has the same chemical makeup and crystal structure as a mined diamond; the only difference is where it was formed. Moissanite is a separate gemstone made of silicon carbide. It looks similar to diamond at a glance, but it is a different mineral, and a jeweler can tell the two apart with a simple tester.

Which one is harder and better for everyday wear?

Diamond is the hardest gemstone, rated 10 on the Mohs scale. Moissanite rates about 9.25, which makes it the next hardest stone commonly used in jewelry. Both are durable enough for a ring you wear every day, and both resist scratching far better than most colored gemstones.

How does their sparkle look different?

A diamond gives bright, crisp white and gray flashes. Moissanite bends light more strongly and splits it into more colored flashes, so it often looks livelier and more rainbow-like, especially in sunlight. Neither look is better; it comes down to personal taste. If you love classic white brilliance, a lab grown diamond is the closer match. If you enjoy a fiery, colorful sparkle, you may enjoy moissanite.

How are they sized and priced?

Moissanite is slightly lighter than diamond, so a moissanite stone is usually described by its diameter in millimeters rather than by carat weight. Lab grown diamonds are described by carat weight, cut, color and clarity. In general, moissanite costs less than a lab grown diamond of a similar visible size, though the exact price depends on the stone and the metal you choose.

How do certificates work?

Lab grown diamonds can be graded and certified by a laboratory. Which should I choose?

Choose a lab grown diamond if you want a stone that is a diamond in every physical sense, with a certificate for the stone. Choose moissanite if you want a larger-looking stone for the money and like colorful sparkle. Both are lab-created, and both can be set in 925 sterling silver, 9K, 14K or 18K gold.

Quick answers

Is moissanite a diamond?

No. Moissanite is silicon carbide, a different mineral from diamond, which is pure carbon.

Is a lab grown diamond a real diamond?

Yes. A lab grown diamond has the same chemical composition and crystal structure as a mined diamond; it is simply grown in a laboratory.

Which is harder, moissanite or diamond?

Diamond is harder, at 10 on the Mohs scale, compared with about 9.25 for moissanite. Both are suitable for everyday wear.

Does moissanite sparkle more than a lab grown diamond?

Moissanite shows more colorful, rainbow-like flashes, while a diamond gives whiter brilliance. Which looks better is a matter of taste.
