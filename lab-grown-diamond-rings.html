Made to order. Order by Dec 10 for Christmas. Free tracked delivery. Gift box included.

Rings questions, answered

Are your diamond rings certified?

Our lab grown diamond rings come with an individual SGL certificate for the diamond in your order. You can verify SGL reports at sgl-labs.com.

Which metals can I choose?

925 sterling silver, 9K gold, 14K gold and 18K gold, each in yellow, white or rose tone. Every option is described by its actual metal; tone finishes are never presented as solid gold.

Do you have lab grown diamond engagement and proposal rings?

Yes. Our four prong solitaires, halo rings and slim pavé band rings are popular as engagement and proposal rings. Each is made to order in Surat in your choice of 925 silver, 9K, 14K or 18K gold, with an SGL certified lab grown diamond. Order early for a proposal date: made to order takes about 5–8 business days plus tracked delivery, and rings cannot be returned or resized.

Can I get a custom ring size?

Yes. Message us your size or measurements on WhatsApp or add a custom size request when you order. Because rings are made to order, they cannot be returned, exchanged or resized.

How long does it take and how is it shipped?

Every piece is made to order and usually ready to ship in about 5–8 business days. Delivery is free worldwide with tracking.
